Why the two panels match

A blur is a linear, shift-invariant operation described by a kernel h. The convolution theorem says its Fourier transform H is the filter's transfer function, and that convolving with h is identical to multiplying by H:

𝒢 = ℱ{f ∗ h} = ℱ{f} · ℱ{h} = F · H

So “smooth each pixel with a weighted average of its neighbours” (spatial) and “scale every frequency by H(u,v) and invert” (frequency) are the same operation. This page runs both and measures the difference: it lands at ~10−12, i.e. floating-point round-off, so the verdict reads identical. See theory.md for the full derivation and experiment.py for the reproducible validation.
